## Why not here

The client's save state is a shared mechanism: it would have to carry every drawable's look (model conditions, sub-object ops of Port 246, the tree fade of Port 266 and this record), not one record. The record and its xfer are ported and tested, so the lane's own part is done; the block belongs to whoever takes the client save state.
